Ways to help

Open

Open

  3. 03

Questions

How much time does it take?
Whatever you can give. Most tasks take an hour or two, and there’s no minimum.
Do I need to know about AI?
No. Teachers, parents and writers are just as useful as people who know AI.
Can high school students help?
Yes. High schoolers can test lessons now and join the tutor list for later.
Can I get volunteer hours?
We’re working on a way to log hours. If your school needs this, mention it in your email.
Will volunteers work with kids directly?
Only tutors, and only after a background check and training. Tutoring hasn’t started yet.

Not sure where you fit?

Email us anyway. Tell us what you’re good at and we’ll find something.
